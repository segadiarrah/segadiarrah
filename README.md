## Current focus — Tauraco

Tauraco has two complementary entry points into one ecosystem:

| Product | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **[Tauraco.io](https://tauraco.io)** | Businesses and accountants | Invoicing, purchasing, accounting, cash management and preparation of financing requests |
| **[Tauraco.ai](https://tauraco.ai)** | Financial institutions | Structured financing applications, asset verification, collateral documentation and evidence |

**Tauraco Collateral** connects business records to assets, verification steps and supporting documents. The aim is to make financing applications easier to assemble, review and trace.

The ERP serves businesses in the OHADA region and Europe. Financing availability depends on the relevant institution, jurisdiction and application. Financing decisions and terms remain with the financial institution; Tauraco is not a lender or guarantor.
